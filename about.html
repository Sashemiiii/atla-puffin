Good sport shouldn't cost a fortune.

We built atla + puffin on a simple idea: great gear should be accessible to everyone. Not just the serious players. Not just the people with premium budgets. Everyone.

The design draws from Scandinavia — clean, functional, honest. Names like Snø, Fjord and Sitron. Paddles with personality. Bags that actually look good. Gear that stands out on any court without standing on anyone's wallet.

Right now, we're all in on pickleball. Paddles, bags, sets — everything you need to get on court. More sports coming soon. Play more. Look good doing it.
